What this version covers

Each paper is placed in the country or countries where its data were collected, and in the ocean basin of the sea it names as its study region, both as read from the full text by a language model. The readings have not been checked by hand. A paper that worked in several countries or basins appears in each. A paper that names no sea has no basin, and a high-seas study may have a basin and no country.

The nine basins follow Shark Oracle's basin columns: the Gulf of Mexico and the Bahamas sit with the Caribbean, and the Black Sea with the Mediterranean.

Species and places are joined at the level of the paper. A paper that covers several species and several countries links every one of its species to every one of its countries, so a species can appear in a place where that paper did not study it; reviews and multi-country studies cause most of this.

Research themes are Shark Oracle's discipline classes; a paper can sit in several. Authors are matched on the name as printed in the reference (surname and initials), so two people who share both count as one author, and one person printed two ways counts as two.

Colouring by species counts the distinct species linked to the papers in each place, within the current filters. Because of the paper-level join described above, treat it as an upper limit.

Species are those Shark Oracle's classification links to each paper. Study sites within a country, sea areas, and distances to port will follow as site-level extraction is added.
