# Team download task

This page used to hold a July 2026 claim list for 1,530 paywalled papers. It is retired; the numbers and paths in it were out of date.

Where to go instead:

- **Downloading hub** (what is left, filter by journal, publisher, or year; shows who has marked what): https://simondedman.github.io/elasmo_analyses/remaining_downloads.html
- **Download helpers** (every remaining paper as a card with search buttons and a "Got it" button; papers with a DOI grouped by publisher, papers without one grouped by journal): https://simondedman.github.io/elasmo_analyses/closed_access/
- **Dashboard and leaderboard**: https://simondedman.github.io/elasmo_analyses/acquisition/remaining_papers.html

Put the PDFs in your named folder on Simon's NAS. Any filename is fine. Email Simon for your link and login.

The helper pages are generated by `scripts/generate_closed_access_html.py` from `docs/papers_data.json`; re-run it after each filing batch so filed papers drop off.
