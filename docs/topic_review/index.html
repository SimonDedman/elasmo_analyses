Topic review

One question, asked one discipline at a time: of every paper the corpus holds, which are genuinely about this? Each topic bundles a discipline's extraction column with the pressure, impact and gear columns that belong to it. A subject expert judges the papers near the line, and the rules are then fitted to those judgements.

First time here? Take the tour (four minutes, then a sandbox where nothing counts).

The three geography topics (habitat, ocean basins, sub-basins) are not disciplines: every paper has a place, so their lists are long and nobody is their champion yet. They are here because the same question still applies to each column.
