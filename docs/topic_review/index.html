Topic review

One question, asked one discipline at a time: of every paper the corpus holds, which are genuinely about this? Each topic bundles a discipline's extraction column with the pressure, impact and gear columns that belong to it. A subject expert judges the papers near the line, and the rules are then fitted to those judgements.
