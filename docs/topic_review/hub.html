How it works

  1. One pass over every PDF counted every candidate keyword, per section, with and without the shark-or-ray proximity filter. That table is the whole input: a rule is only a way of adding the counts up.
  2. So a change to a threshold, a section weight, a keyword, or the proximity filter re-scores every paper in the browser, immediately. No corpus re-run.
  3. The reviewer judges papers against a written definition of the concept. Papers well above the line and well below it are spot-checked at random; the rest of the reading time goes on the papers near it.
  4. Once a topic has labels, the search in the Optimiser tab proposes the rule that reproduces them best and reports accuracy on held-out labels, so reviewers never need to tune numbers by hand.
