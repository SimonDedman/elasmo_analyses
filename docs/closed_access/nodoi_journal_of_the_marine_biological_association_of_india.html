How to use this page

  1. Pick your name under You and, if you like, set your own library's search link.
  2. For each paper try Google Scholar first (look for an [PDF] link on the right), then Google restricted to PDFs, then BHL for older and taxonomic work, then JSTOR and your library. Where there is a citation line under the title, it gives the volume and pages as Shark-References records them.
  3. If you get the PDF, save it and click Got it. That greys it out for everyone, here and on the downloading hub. Clicking a search button records nothing, so a dead end costs nobody anything.
  4. If you can't get it, click Can't get it. That only marks it on your own browser, so you don't try it twice; someone with different access may still succeed.
  5. Put the PDFs in your named folder on Simon's NAS. Any filename is fine. No NAS link yet? Email Simon and he'll send one.
