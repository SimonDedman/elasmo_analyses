Regional summaries and the Species Atlas

Shark Oracle is planning a map of where chondrichthyan research has been done, species by species, and a summary page for each region. This page records the plan and how far it has got.

What we are building

Species Atlas. A map with a marker on each study site, so you can zoom to a region and see which species have been studied there, by whom, and in which papers. A first version maps papers by country and ocean basin.

Regional summaries. One page per region that gathers the papers, species, topics, and threats studied there, kept current as new papers arrive and curated by a regional champion. They will sit beside the per-species summaries, and the map will be the way in to both.

Sensitive sites. Locations of aggregation sites, pupping grounds, and similar sites of threatened species will be shown at country or ocean-basin level by default.

What papers print about where they worked

We read a stratified sample of 120 papers and recorded what each one states about its study sites. Shares are weighted to the 21,013 papers with readable text.

Most papers name a place (82%) and few print coordinates (18%), so the atlas will rest mainly on matching place names to a gazetteer, with printed coordinates as the precise minority.

Indicative figures from a small sample, read by a language model (Claude Fable 5.2) and not yet checked by hand. The ranges are approximate. Built 2026-11-12.
