EEA 2026 SIDE EVENT SUBMISSION, draft text for the Google form
Simon Dedman, 2026-08-27

TITLE
Ask the literature anything: a searchable database of elasmobranch research,
and rescuing the conference record

FORMAT
Interactive presentation and discussion, led by Simon Dedman (Florida
International University). 30 to 45 minutes. No technical background needed.

DESCRIPTION (main form field)

At EEA 2025 in Rotterdam we ran a panel on data and analysis methods in
elasmobranch research. It has since grown into elasmo_analyses, an effort to
build one searchable database of the field: every paper we can obtain, with
species, methods, geography, pressures, disciplines, and authorship extracted
from each, feeding into Sharkipedia and IUCN Red List assessments.

This session is a progress report and, more importantly, a conversation about
what the resource should become. I will spend roughly half the time
demonstrating where the project has reached, including a live walk through the
database and the query interface, and half on discussion.

A particular focus is the conference abstract archive. Conference abstracts sit
in no bibliographic database, yet they hold decades of work that never reached
publication, including a great deal of student research. Working with the Shark
Trust, the American Elasmobranch Society, and the Oceania Chondrichthyan
Society, we have so far assembled over 12,000 abstracts spanning 1985 to 2026
across four conference series, ten EEA meetings among them. Several more EEA
years are in hand and being processed, so by October the EEA back catalogue
should be searchable for the first time.

The discussion is the real point of the session. What would you want to ask a
database like this? What would make it genuinely useful for your own work,
teaching, or assessments? What should the EEA and its member groups get back
from it? Please bring the questions you have never been able to answer because
the evidence was scattered across too many papers, and the gaps you know exist
in the historical record.
